Global End-use Electrical Equipment Market Model, 2026–2035

About this project: a bottom-up model of the global end-use electrical equipment market to 2035, built from IEA electricity demand data and equipment cost benchmarks, with adjustable scenarios. Model estimates global electrical equipment market size by end-use/sector, equipment used to serve new demand (growth market) and equipment used to replace existing demand (replacement market).

Personal project. Views and estimates are my own.

Market by sector ($B)

Key takeaways

  • Prioritize electrical equipment for AI facilities, such as data centers: data centers drive the market, with almost half of the 2026 market and most of the incremental growth (59%) by 2035.
  • Grow replacement and services business: a 31% share by 2035, growing 5.8% annually (steadier than new-build demand). This is an opportunity to build or expand service, retrofit and spare-parts businesses alongside winning new projects.

Base-case figures; the sliders above change the scenario but not this text.

Method and assumptions

  • Only includes electrical equipment for end-uses. Does not include utility generation, transmission & distribution.
  • Bottom-up build: IEA electricity demand by end-use sector (TWh) → convert to capacity (MW) using sector capacity factors → × electrical equipment cost per MW.
  • New growth equipment = added MW × cost. Replacement = 2% of installed MW a year × cost (rate adjustable above).
  • Total demand grows 3.6% a year to 2030, then rises by a constant 1,080 TWh a year to 2035 (conservative flat-rate assumption).

Sliders recompute the sector model; replacement is not re-based when additions change.
